Senkisem I. Free

Notes from
a Stranger

“It’s enough if you just feel something stir inside you.”

This isn’t a self-help course, and it isn’t an interrogation. It’s a stranger who signs as Nobody, sitting next to you with a notebook, asking the questions no one has asked you in a long time. What do you wish others just knew about you? What life would you live if nobody’s opinion mattered?

Every question is followed by a page that answers back, and then by pages that are simply yours. 49 of them are blank, and you can write on them right in your browser. Your answers never leave your device.

Senkisem II. Free

Synthetic
Human

“Your personality is not who you are.”

This is not self-improvement. This is excavation. The book opens with a failed login and never lets you read from the outside. The character you play, the program you learned as a child, the need for something bigger: all of it is code you didn’t write, but are still running.

Written like a system log, with status panels, alerts and diagnostics, it reads you back. It ends at an Exit Point with four options: Reset. Remain. Observe. Or refuse to choose, which, the book will tell you, is a choice too.

Senkisem I.

Start here if you want to be asked.

Soft, direct, human. Questions, silence, and pages to write in. It doesn’t tell you who you are. It waits until you do.

Senkisem II.

Cold, clinical, relentless. A system log that takes the character apart piece by piece and leaves the decision to you.

Or read both. They are two halves of the same idea.
